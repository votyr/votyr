<tr>

<div align="center">

### ✦ I · NYAYADEEP ✦

#### *Learning for All*

❧

*An interactive study of the Indian Constitution.*

NyayaDeep was conceived as a place where constitutional knowledge could become easier to approach and explore. Dense material is transformed into a structured learning experience, with interactive elements designed to make the subject less intimidating and more engaging.

<br/>

`MERN` · `Education` · `Interactive Web`

*❧ A digital library for the curious mind.*

</div>

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

<div align="center">

### ✦ II · CRAVE ✦

#### *An AI-Powered Nutrition Companion*

❧

*An experiment at the meeting point of food, intelligence, and personalisation.*

Crave explores how artificial intelligence can make nutrition feel more personal and approachable. The project brings together an interactive web experience and data-driven recommendations in an attempt to make healthy choices feel less like a duty and more like a discovery.

<br/>

`React` · `Flask` · `PostgreSQL` · `AI`

*❧ A study in intelligent, personal software.*

</div>

</td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

<div align="center">

### ✦ III · SCAM2SAFE ✦

#### *The Watchful Companion*

❧

*A small defence against the dangers hidden in ordinary messages.*

Scam2Safe is a privacy-first system for recognising suspicious digital interactions. It examines common warning signs and produces an explainable risk assessment, while its architecture leaves room for future AI-assisted detection.

<br/>

`MERN` · `React Native` · `REST API` · `Security`

*❧ Built for the unwary traveller of the digital world.*

</div>

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

<div align="center">

### ✦ IV · TASK MANAGER ✦

#### *An Engine for Order*

❧

*A humble instrument for imposing order upon the chaos of the day.*

This full-stack application explores the foundations of a practical productivity system: authentication, persistent data, API-driven workflows, and a simple interface for turning scattered responsibilities into something more manageable.

<br/>

`Full-Stack` · `REST API` · `Database`

*❧ A modest remedy for disorder.*

</div>

</td>
